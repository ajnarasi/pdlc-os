Architecture · 06 · why this is defensible

Three load-bearing primitives.

01 · agent loop

Schema-enforced

Every stage uses Anthropic's forced tool_use. The schema is the contract, not a suggestion.

02 · audit ribbon

Signed

SHA chain back to Discovery. Every artifact carries {stage, agent, eval_score, citations[], hash, parent_hashes[], timestamp}.

03 · panel review

7 perspectives

Executive · Engineer · Designer · UXR · Customer Voice · Legal · Skeptic. Runs on any stage on demand. ~10 seconds in parallel.

Regulator-ready by default. Compliance-ready by default.
